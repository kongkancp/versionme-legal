How do I restore my purchase?
Go to Profile → Upgrade to Vex Pro → Restore Purchase.
Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID
used when you originally subscribed or purchased.

Is Vex Pro a subscription?
Yes. Vex Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks
AI coaching features:
  • Monthly: $4.99/month
  • Yearly: $39.99/year (save 33%)
Both plans include a 14-day free trial.
All core features — goals, missions, levels, and progress
tracking — are completely free with no subscription required.

I previously paid $4.99 for VersionMe. Do I get Pro?
Yes. If you purchased the original one-time $4.99
in-app purchase, you automatically receive Vex Pro
access permanently at no additional charge.
Tap Restore Purchase to activate it.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → VersionMe.
Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date
to avoid being charged for the next period.

My progress disappeared. What happened?
Your data is stored on your device and synced to your
private iCloud account if you are signed in to iCloud.
Deleting and reinstalling the app may cause data loss
if iCloud sync is not enabled.
To protect your data:
  • Stay signed in to iCloud
  • Do not delete the app unnecessarily
  • Check Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → VersionMe is ON

Can I use VersionMe on multiple devices?
Yes. VersionMe syncs your goals and progress across
all your Apple devices using iCloud.
Sign in with the same Apple ID on each device and
your data will sync automatically.

What is Vex AI Coach?
Vex is your personal AI coach, available with Vex Pro.
Vex can:
  • Chat about your goals and give data-driven advice
  • Send you a weekly coaching report every Monday
  • Suggest better missions when one feels too hard or easy
  • Generate a monthly analysis of your progress
Vex uses the Claude API by Anthropic to generate
personalized responses based on your goal data.

How does mission generation work?
When you add a goal, VersionMe creates a personalized
mission plan based on your skill level, available time,
and target duration. For custom goals you create yourself,
AI generates a complete 5-level plan tailored to your input.
Missions range from foundation to milestone across 10 days
per level, with progressive difficulty throughout.

How do levels work?
Each goal has 5 levels, each containing 10 unique missions.
Complete missions to progress through levels.
Each mission type has a purpose:
  Foundation → builds the habit
  Exploration → varies the approach
  Recovery → rest and reflect
  Building → increases difficulty
  Challenge → pushes your limits
  Milestone → achieves something new

Why did my mission change?
If you rated a mission as too hard or too easy
multiple times, Vex automatically suggested an
adjusted version better suited to your current level.
This is the Adaptive Mission feature working as intended.

Does the app access my health data?
No. VersionMe does not access Apple Health, HealthKit,
or any fitness tracker data. All progress is tracked
manually through mission completions and check-ins.

Your privacy is important to us.

Data stored on your device and iCloud:
  • Your goals, missions, and progress
  • Streak and completion history
  • Reflections and outcome check-ins
  • All personal data stays in your private iCloud account

Data sent to AI (Vex Pro only):
  When using Vex AI Coach, limited data is sent
  to Anthropic's API to generate your coaching response:
  • Your first name
  • Goal title and current level
  • Streak and weekly completion rate
  • Skill level and difficulty ratings
  No health data, location, or device identifiers are shared.

When you create a custom goal:
  Your goal description text is sent to Anthropic's API
  to generate your personalized mission plan.
  Do not include sensitive personal information
  in your goal descriptions.
