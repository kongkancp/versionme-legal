VERSIONME — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. OVERVIEW
VersionMe is designed with privacy in mind. We aim to collect only the data needed to provide the service. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your email address.

2. INFORMATION WE HANDLE

2.1 Information you provide
- Your first name
- Goals you create, including titles, life areas and descriptions
- Answers and anchors you type when setting up a goal
- Mood check-ins
- Reflection journal entries and weekly plans
- Outcome values and custom metrics you track

2.2 Information generated by your use of the app
- Mission completion records, streaks, levels and difficulty ratings
- App usage dates
- Your subscription status
- A random installation identifier created on your device. It is not linked to your name or Apple ID and is used to help limit excessive use of AI features.

2.3 Information we do not ask for
We do not ask for your email address, account credentials, location, HealthKit or fitness tracker data, advertising identifiers, or your contacts.

2.4 Health-related information
Some goals relate to health, fitness, sleep or mental wellbeing, and you may choose to write health-related details in your goals, reflections, plans or metrics. Please do not enter information you would not want stored in your iCloud account or processed by the AI service described below. VersionMe is not a medical service (see our Health Disclaimer).

3. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

3.1 On your device and in iCloud
Your data is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and sync is on, it is also stored in your private iCloud account through Apple CloudKit so that it is available on your other devices.

3.2 AI features
When you use AI features (such as coaching reports, mission adjustment, cycle insights and custom goal creation), information from your goals and progress is sent to an AI service provider to generate a response. Depending on the feature, this can include your first name, goal titles and areas, level, streak and completion rates, mission details, difficulty ratings, outcome metrics, and text you wrote yourself, such as reflection notes, weekly plans, goal descriptions and habit anchors. Your mood check-ins are not sent.

3.3 Service providers
We use the following kinds of service providers to run VersionMe:
- An AI provider (currently Anthropic) that generates AI responses.
- An infrastructure provider (currently Cloudflare) that hosts a relay service which passes AI requests from the app to the AI provider.
- Apple, for iCloud sync and App Store subscriptions.
These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies, which govern how long they keep it and what they may use it for. Typical technical information that accompanies any internet request, such as an IP address, may be visible to them. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

3.4 Subscriptions
Vex Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through the Apple App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; we do not receive your card or payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple to unlock Pro features.

4. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
Your data remains on your device and in your iCloud account until you delete it.
- Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. If iCloud sync is on, your data remains in your iCloud account and may reappear if you reinstall the app on a device signed in to the same Apple ID.
- To delete the iCloud copy, go to Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → VersionMe → Delete Data.
- Information already sent to service providers is handled under their policies.

5. CHILDREN
VersionMe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us.

6. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS
You can control and delete your data as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information, such as access, correction, deletion or objection. You can contact us at any time with questions or requests.

7. SECURITY
We use reasonable measures to protect information, including on-device storage, Apple's iCloud protections, and encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you through the app.
